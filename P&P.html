SERENE SPACES POLICY

Last Updated: June 2024

Our Design Philosophy

At Serene Spaces, we believe privacy should be as carefully curated as your living space. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our design services and marketplace.

Design Principle: We only collect what's necessary to deliver exceptional service, and we treat your data with the same respect we'd expect for our own.

Data We Collect

To create your ideal design experience, we may collect:

Category Purpose Examples
Design Profile Personalize your experience Style preferences, room dimensions
Transaction Process purchases Billing address, payment method
Communication Provide customer support Email, chat transcripts
Usage Improve our services Page views, click patterns

How We Use Your Information

3.1 Service Delivery

To process transactions, recommend products matching your design aesthetic, and provide customer support.

3.2 Personalization

To curate product selections based on your style preferences and previous purchases.

3.3 Communications

To send order confirmations, design inspiration, and (with consent) our newsletter.

Sharing With Care

We only share data when necessary:

  • Design Partners: For custom orders with our artisans
  • Shipping Providers: To deliver your purchases
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law

We never sell your personal information to third-party marketers.

Design Tools (Cookies)

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Remember your design preferences
  • Keep items in your cart
  • Understand how clients use our site

You can manage preferences in your browser settings.

Your Design Rights

You may:

  • Access or update your personal information
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt-out of marketing communications
  • Request a copy of your data

Contact us at privacy@serenespaces.com to exercise these rights.

Secure Design

We implement industry-standard measures to protect your information:

  • Encrypted transactions (SSL/TLS)
  • Regular security audits
  • Limited employee access

Evolving Standards

We may update this policy as design standards evolve. Significant changes will be communicated through our newsletter or your account dashboard.
